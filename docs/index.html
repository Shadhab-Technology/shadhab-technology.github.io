JobTrack is an Android app for counters that take items in and hand them back. Built first for tailoring and alterations; the same take-in, track, hand-back flow fits dry cleaners and repair counters. Item fields are tailored to garments today. Runs on a SUNMI V2 or any Android 7+ phone or tablet.

Benefits

Why use it

  • No more lost tickets. Every job is recorded with a reference and due date.
  • See the counter at a glance. What is due, in progress or ready.
  • Find any job in seconds. Search by name, phone or reference.
  • Money in pounds. Prices, amount paid and balance are recorded for each job.
  • Itemised receipts. Print with a QR code that opens the order (on SUNMI).
  • Works without internet. The core workflow needs no connection.
  • Encrypted on the device. Customer data is encrypted where it is stored.
  • Backups you control. Encrypted, exported by you, restored when you need them.
  • Free to start. Free download, no subscription needed to begin.
  • Runs on what you have. A SUNMI counter device or any Android 7+ phone or tablet.

How to install

You need an Android 7+ device. Pick one route:

Before relying on customer records, save an encrypted backup and check that you can restore it.

  1. Direct APKOpen the latest release on your device, download the APK, open it and allow installs from your browser or files app when Android asks.
  2. ObtainiumIn the free Obtainium app, add the same releases page as a source and it will install JobTrack and tell you about new versions.
