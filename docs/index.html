Android app for tailors

Every alteration job, in one place.

JobTrack is an Android app for tailoring and alteration workshops. Record the customer, the job, the garments and the work agreed, then follow each job through to collection. It runs on a SUNMI V2 counter device or any Android 7+ phone or tablet.

Features

What it does

01

Customers, jobs, garments and alterations

Each job has a reference and a due date, with its garments and alteration instructions together.

02

Status tracking

Move jobs from received, to in progress, to ready, to collected, so you can see what needs doing and what is waiting.

03

Prices and receipts

Record the price and amount paid in pounds, and print an itemised receipt on the SUNMI's built-in printer with a QR code that opens the order.

04

Quick search

Find a job by customer name, phone or job reference.

05

Works offline

The core workflow needs no live internet connection, and the data on the device is encrypted.

06

Encrypted backups

Save a password-protected backup off the device and restore it when you need to. Backups are exported by you; they are not sent offsite automatically.
