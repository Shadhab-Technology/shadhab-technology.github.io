JobTrack · Shadhab Technology

Every alteration job, in one place.

JobTrack is an Android app for tailoring and alteration workshops. Record the customer, the job, the garments and the work agreed, then follow each job through to collection. It runs on a SUNMI V2 counter device or any Android 7+ phone or tablet.

Download for Android

What it does

  • Customers, jobs, garments and alterations. Each job has a reference and a due date, with its garments and alteration instructions together.
  • Status tracking. Move jobs from received, to in progress, to ready, to collected, so you can see what needs doing and what is waiting.
  • Prices and receipts. Record the price and amount paid in pounds, and print an itemised receipt on the SUNMI's built-in printer with a QR code that opens the order.
  • Quick search. Find a job by customer name, phone or job reference.
  • Works offline. The core workflow needs no live internet connection, and the data on the device is encrypted.
  • Encrypted backups. Save a password-protected backup off the device and restore it when you need to. Backups are exported by you; they are not sent offsite automatically.

How to install

You need an Android 7+ device. Pick one route:

  1. Direct APK. Open the latest release on your device, download the APK, open it and allow installs from your browser or files app when Android asks.
  2. Obtainium. In the free Obtainium app, add the same releases page as a source and it will install JobTrack and tell you about new versions.

Before relying on customer records, save an encrypted backup and check that you can restore it.
